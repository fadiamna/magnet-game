🧲 What is a Magnet?

A magnet is an object with special power to attract only certain types of metals, not all of them! It has two poles: North (N) and South (S)

🔩 Metals attracted to magnets:

• Iron (like nails and keys)

• Steel (like kitchen utensils)

• Nickel (in some coins)

• Cobalt (rare metal)

❌ Metals NOT attracted to magnets:

Gold, Silver, Copper, Aluminum, Lead

⚡ The Golden Rule:

Opposite poles attract each other ❤️
Like poles repel each other ⚡

💡 Uses of Magnets:

Compass 🧭 • Refrigerator doors 🏠 • Smartphones 📱
Magnetic trains 🚄 • Computers 💻 • Electric generators ⚡
